Generated by `tools/catalogue_md.lua` from the engine itself. Do not edit by hand.

Nothing in the catalogue is stored. Every entry below is calculated when it is
asked for, from the scale, the chords, the instrument and the register, then
checked the way a player would check it. The lists here are what that makes
for one reference setting: **C major, I-V-vi-IV, four bars of 4/4 at 120 bpm,
middle register**. Change any of those and the notes change with it, and so,
sometimes, does what is left out.

What each instrument does not play, and why:

- **Timpani**, Alberti: An Alberti figure needs three notes; the timpani have two.
- **Timpani**, Arpeggio: An arpeggio needs more notes than the timpani are tuned to.
- **Timpani**, Broken chord: A broken chord needs more notes than the timpani are tuned to.
- **Timpani**, Harmony: Timpani are tuned to the tonic and the dominant; they hold the bass of the harmony, not its voices.
- **Timpani**, Melody: Timpani are tuned before they play, to a few notes, so a melody is not theirs to play.
- **Glockenspiel**, Cluster: A cluster here is three or four notes at once; with two mallets this instrument plays two.
