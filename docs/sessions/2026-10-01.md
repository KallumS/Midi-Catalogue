# Session log - 2026-10-01

## What was asked

First, the REAPER API functions page, the ReaScript page and the user guide
were uploaded "before I test". Then, after testing: "GUI works well but is very
busy" - could the instruments take less space? - and "there aren't enough
chords": every chord, as in Starting Blocks, chained into progressions.

## What was done

**The API check.** Every `reaper.` call was checked against the uploaded API
page (REAPER 7.79). All signatures matched; the meanings that matter were
recorded in `CLAUDE.md` (`IP_TRACKNUMBER` is 1-based, `InsertTrackAtIndex`
0-based, `StuffMIDIMessage` mode 0 is the virtual keyboard). Nothing changed in
the code.

**Every chord, chained** ([0009](../decisions/0009-chords-are-a-chain-of-any-chord.md)).
Starting Blocks' chord tables went into `mc_theory` unchanged. The progression
became a chain of up to eight links, each a degree and any chord. The old
progressions are presets. The Triads/Sevenths switch went: the Diatonic family
covers it.

**The scale bends to the chord.** The first try with a borrowed C major chord
in C minor showed the problem: the engine writes in scale steps, so "the third"
of that chord came out Eb. `T.chordKey` gives each chord the scale as it sounds
under it, and every pitch in the catalogue is now read through the scale at
its moment. The demo output then read as a player would write it: the violin's
arch over I-iv-V7-i in C minor plays E natural over the borrowed I, Eb again
over iv, B natural over G7.

**A smaller window** ([0010](../decisions/0010-show-a-family-at-a-time.md)).
Instruments a family at a time, the register beside the families; the chord
editor only while a chord is open.

## What the tests found

- The UI test's "+" check counted "IV" buttons, and the open editor has a "IV"
  degree button too. It reads the chain from the block's name now.
- "Strings" is a family and a section; the test picks the later button.
- Switching chord scales off was not caught by the catalogue suite - melodies
  just avoided the borrowed note. A named check now requires E natural over a
  borrowed C major in C minor, in an arpeggio, an ostinato and the violin's
  arches.
- The test expecting 69 named chords was wrong: Starting Blocks has 78.
- The "nothing clashes with a borrowed chord" rule tripped on the
  Anticipation rhythm, which plays the next chord half a beat early on
  purpose; it is exempt, like the pedal.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 11,656 theory, about 3.35 million catalogue, 46 MIDI, 68
placement and 240 window checks; `docs/CATALOGUE.md` up to date, now with the
chord table.

## Left open

- Each chord in a chain gets an equal share of the bars. A chord held twice as
  long as the next is a later question.
- Chords on roots outside the scale (bVI, bVII in major) are not offered: a
  root is always a degree of the scale.
