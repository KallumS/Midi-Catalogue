# Session log - 2026-09-30

The first session. The repo started empty but for its licence.

## What was asked

A plugin for a different way of producing: picking MIDI clips from a
catalogue. RapidComposer comes closest but is "far too arcane" with too many
menus; Starting Blocks was the first sketch of the idea and this is to be the
full version. Other tools are "too random and not musical" - this should give
ideas musicians would actually use in a track.

Pick a scale first, then browse by category - Rhythm (Ostinato, Pulse,
Syncopation, Gallop, Tresillo, Hemiola, Polyrhythm, Arpeggio, Repeated note,
Alberti, Broken chord), Melody (Ascending, Descending, Arch, Wave, Sequence,
Call/response, Repetition, Development), Harmony (Triadic, Quartal, Cluster,
Pedal, Arpeggiated, Contrary motion). Generated, not pre-loaded, "because music
is math". Idiomatic to each orchestral instrument - ask for Violin I, get
Violin I parts; ask for oboe, get oboe parts - with Orchestration Helper as a
source. A ReaScript or a JSFX. All velocity at 100, and aware enough to output
rhythmic motifs, ostinatos, melodic contours, chord patterns, orchestration,
voice leading, repetitions, transformations, density, register and velocity
patterns.

Starting Blocks, ScaleView for REAPER, Midi Suggester, Midi Variator and
Orchestration Helper were pointed to as prior work.

## What was built

A Lua ReaScript ([0001](../decisions/0001-a-reascript-not-a-jsfx.md)) in the
shape of the sister repos: three pure music files, a REAPER layer, a ReaImGui
window, and tests for each.

- `mc_theory` - ScaleView's keys and scales (via Starting Blocks, asserted
  against ScaleView's values), spelling, numerals, eleven progressions named in
  the scale's own numerals, chords stacked from the scale, and a beam-search
  voice leader.
- `mc_orchestra` - 25 instruments and 4 sections, each with a range, a sweet
  register, a role in four-part harmony, hands, speed in seconds, widest leap,
  breath, and what it does not play
  ([0008](../decisions/0008-an-instrument-is-range-speed-leap-breath-hands.md)).
- `mc_catalogue` - the 25 types as lists of musical choices, every combination
  generated and checked ([0002](../decisions/0002-calculated-from-choices-not-stored-or-random.md)),
  transformations, repeats, accents, density, and the block.
- `mc_midi` - Starting Blocks' writer plus format 1 for sections.
- `mc_place` - insert on the selected track, or a new track per instrument for
  a section; export; audition.
- The window: 1 Scale, 2 Chords underneath, 3 Instrument, 4 Catalogue, then
  Shape it, a preview roll, Insert / Export / Audition.

The user's "every possibility" was read as every combination of the musically
meaningful choices, filtered by the rules, with what is left out counted and
explained - the only version of the request that produces music rather than
noise. "All velocity at 100" and "velocity patterns" both hold: 100 by
default, accents above it on request
([0005](../decisions/0005-velocity-100-accents-above-it.md)).

## What reading the output found

`tools/demo.lua` prints every entry as note names, and reading it drove most
of the melody work.

**The first melodies stalled.** Sampling the contour at every note and rounding
gave D5 D5 F5 F5 wherever the line rose slowly. **The second trilled**: a path
search that priced only steps and repeats found C D C D C D free. The third,
kept, puts a skeleton on the strong beats and searches the path between over
pairs of notes, so a change of direction and a return to the note before last
can be priced ([0003](../decisions/0003-melody-is-a-skeleton-then-a-path.md)).
An "ascending" skeleton could still dip after snapping to the chord, so the
skeleton now never goes back against the contour.

**Motif restatements bent one note.** Moving only a statement's first note onto
the chord gave B4 B4 C5 D5. Whole statements move now.

**Cadences repeated their last note** (F5 F5). They approach home from the
chord tone above.

**Two-bar rhythms were cut in half.** The clave and the long tresillo restart
on each chord, and with one chord a bar they lost their second bar and came out
identical to the one-bar tresillo. A cell longer than a chord now runs straight
through.

## What the tests found

- **The interval repair moved downbeats off the chord.** Removing an augmented
  second or a tritone moved "the weaker note", which could be a strong one when
  both were. Strong notes are never moved now.
- **Breathing re-struck a note under the wrong chord.** A tuba note held over a
  bar line came back after the breath under a chord it does not belong to. It
  comes back only if it fits.
- **An anticipation overlapped the next one** in a one-bar block with a chord
  a beat: eight notes at once on the piano. Pushes are capped.
- **Arpeggios started on the fifth.** The first chord starts from its root now.
- **The timpani's inversion left a drum under a chord without it.** Inversion
  swaps the drums and then checks the chord.
- **Register ties went to the lower octave** because the octave search ran from
  the bottom. It runs from no shift outwards, and a shift has to be clearly
  better to win.
- **Four horns in one range had no voicing**, and brass quartal chords none:
  the horns got the high and low halves of the compass, and quartal voicings
  share a band like clusters.
- **Glockenspiel and xylophone had empty harmony types.** Two mallets cannot
  play four-note chords; they are marked as not playing them, with the reason.
- **The leading-tone test passed with the rule removed.** It checked one chord
  pair. It now audits every voiced chord of every progression.
- **Accents on harmony accented every chord.** They are on the downbeats now.
- One test was wrong, not the code: D Dorian's sixth degree is diminished, so
  "i-VI-III-VII" reads i-vi°-III-VII there.

## Numbers

`tools/test.sh`: 11,125 theory checks; the catalogue suite's 219 rules over
about 2.85 million individual checks; 46 MIDI, 68 placement and 206 window
checks; `docs/CATALOGUE.md` up to date. About twenty seconds.

## Left open

- Nothing here has been run inside REAPER yet. The window is checked against a
  mock that raises on any ReaImGui function it lacks, so a missing function
  fails in the test, but layout and spacing can only be judged on screen.
- The instrument numbers (sweet registers, speeds, leaps) are standard values
  and judgement. They are one table in `mc_orchestra.lua` if a player
  disagrees.
- Unpitched percussion is left to Starting Blocks.
